About Us

The UK Top Songs is an unofficial, fan-made version of the Official Charts Company's Official UK Singles Chart, removing the ACR and 3-single rules and instead implementing a recurrency rule, similar to Billboard. The chart was created in mid-2024 as a fun project, an alternate way to view the chart. We are not affiliated with OCC. Charts are usually published on a Friday, though this can vary.

UK TOP SONGS CHART RULES

As of August 2026

Tracking Week

Like OCC, the tracking week is Friday 12.00am > Thursday 11.59pm

Physical vs Digital Sales

Each physical and digital sale of a song is worth 1 chart sale.

Paid vs Free Streams

Like OCC, songs will have a streaming to sales ratio of 100:1 for paid streams, and 600:1 for free streams.

Unlike OCC, we do not half the streaming units after three weeks of decline in streams, relative to the combined streaming market rate of change for those weeks (also known as ACR).

Album Cuts

There is no limit on the amount of tracks each artist can chart at once.

Like OCC, songs that are shorter than 30 seconds, or longer than 15 minutes, will not be eligible to chart.

Any number of remixes tied to a specific song will be counted under the main song title by default, however this can change if a remix is definitively the more popular version of the track.

Chart Manipulation

Songs that have been purchased or streamed in a way other than genuine will have their manipulated sales figures removed as best as possible. We strive for a fair chart, manipulating it in a way that is considered to unfairly influence chart positions will not be allowed.

Recurrency

Recurrency is the act of removing a track from the chart based on its current position and number of weeks on the chart.

Recurrency only occurs if a song declines in position and units.

Recurrency occurs:

  • When a song falls below #5 with at least 78 weeks on the chart
  • When a song falls below #10 with at least 52 weeks on the chart
  • When a song falls below #25 with at least 26 weeks on the chart
  • When a song falls below #50 with at least 20 weeks on the chart

Songs are eligible to return to the chart IF:

  • They hit a new positional peak
  • They hit a new sales peak
  • They reach the above threshold (e.g. a song falling out the top 50 reaching the top 25)
  • They return to the threshold they fell out of after 26 weeks.

Recurrency rules do not occur during chart weeks that commence during December.

Christmas Music

Songs that are tagged as "Christmas" are only eligible to chart during December.

AI

Songs that feature provable use of Generative Artificial Intelligence, regardless of what it was used for, will be exempt from charting, although this is taken on a case-by-case basis. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Modifying your voice by use of AI to be the voice of someone else
  • Generating instrumental elements with AI
  • Generating full songs with AI
  • Generating lyrics with AI

We want to try to remove as little as we can, and will try and give artists the benefit of the doubt where possible, however artists who have endorsed or used AI will be more likely to have music excluded, regardless of the weight of evidence against them.

This does not extend to artwork on cover arts or music videos, although we will not endorse these either.
